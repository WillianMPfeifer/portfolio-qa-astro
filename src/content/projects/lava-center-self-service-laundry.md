## Context

Landing page project developed as a business model for a modern self-service laundromat (Lava Center), focusing on convenience, clarity, and local customer acquisition.

## Problem

Self-service laundromats rely heavily on local discovery and answering customer questions upfront (how self-service works, cycle duration, pricing, and detergent availability). A lightweight, mobile-first interface was needed to turn nearby searchers into customers.

## Decisions

- **Semantic and lightweight build:** Built in HTML5 with Tailwind CSS to ensure instant load times on mobile devices.
- **Clarity and UX:** Implemented a 3-step illustrated walkthrough for first-time customers, transparent pricing tables, and an interactive FAQ powered by lightweight JavaScript.
- **Local SEO & Schema.org:** Integrated structured JSON-LD data (`Laundromat` / `LocalBusiness`) including geolocation coordinates, opening hours, and pricing ranges for enhanced Google Maps and search rankings.
- **Direct conversion points:** One-click WhatsApp contact and route guidance buttons.

## Outcome

A responsive, high-speed landing page optimized for local discovery and customer onboarding.
