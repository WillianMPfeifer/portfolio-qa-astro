## Context

A business landing page developed for Lava Center, a modern self-service laundromat, designed to drive local walk-ins and explain the self-service model clearly to new customers.

## Problem

Express laundromats rely on local proximity searches and immediate clarity: first-time visitors need to quickly understand the payment methods, cycle times, detergent policies, and pricing before visiting. The challenge was building an ultra-fast, mobile-optimized experience that ranks high on Google Maps and search queries.

## Decisions

- **Semantic, Lightweight Markup:** Built with semantic HTML5 and Tailwind CSS, delivering instant rendering and top Lighthouse performance on mobile devices.
- **Customer Walkthrough & UX:** Designed an illustrated 3-step guide explaining the wash-and-dry process, clear per-cycle pricing tables, and a lightweight interactive FAQ.
- **Local SEO & Schema.org:** Embedded JSON-LD structured data (`Laundromat` / `LocalBusiness`) with precise geo-coordinates, operating hours, and service catalogs to maximize Google Local Pack visibility.
- **High-Intent Conversion:** Integrated direct WhatsApp chat triggers and Google Maps route navigation.

## Outcome

A responsive, high-converting digital storefront that accelerates local customer acquisition and automates answers to common customer questions.
